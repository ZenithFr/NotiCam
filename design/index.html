Interactive mock of the shipping Jetpack Compose screen (CameraScreen.kt + ui/components/*). Everything below is clickable. Tuned for the CMF Phone 2 Pro at 1080 × 2392.

What the redesign changes

  • System yellow selection. Mode names and the ƒ dial are the only coloured elements — the old Nothing-red accents and neon pills are gone.
  • Glass, not cards. 55% #1C1C1E capsules with a 0.5dp hairline instead of the heavy acrylic slabs and full-width dark bars.
  • Real Apple shutter. 4dp ring + gap + disc, compresses on press, ring pulse + haptic on capture.
  • Never over the scene. Top bar, lens chips, ƒ row and shutter row are laid out outside the 4:3 framebuffer in one column, so no control can ever sit on top of the image. Open Pro and watch the frame give up height instead of being covered.
  • No stretch, no crop. The sensor streams 1440 × 1080; the box is measured to exactly 4:3 and the composite shader letterboxes as a second guarantee.
  • One-tap reach. Flash, Pro sheet, tap-to-focus, pinch-zoom and the ƒ ladder all live where the thumb already is.

Try

  • Tap the viewfinder → yellow AE/AF reticle (drives focusAt()).
  • Press the shutter → bloom, ring pulse, and the frame flies into the gallery.
  • Switch to Portrait → the ƒ ladder appears in the bottom bar.
  • Open Pro → glass sheet slides up, button becomes a white ✕.
